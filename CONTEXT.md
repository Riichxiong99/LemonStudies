# LemonStudies

A Qt/QML desktop study app combining a Pomodoro timer, distraction blocking, a to-do list, and streak tracking.

## Language

**Pomodoro session**:
One run of the timer from Start through the work interval and its break, ending automatically back at Idle (or cut short via Stop).
_Avoid_: pomodoro, cycle, round

**Music Link**:
A saved, user-labeled YouTube URL — a single video or a playlist — that can be selected to play as background audio during a Pomodoro session. Playlist entries play in sequence and loop back to the start if the session outlasts the playlist.
_Avoid_: track, playlist entry, song
