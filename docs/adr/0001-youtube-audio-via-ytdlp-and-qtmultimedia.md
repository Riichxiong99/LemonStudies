# Play study music by resolving YouTube links through yt-dlp into QtMultimedia

To play music from a YouTube link inside the app, we resolve a direct audio-stream URL with `yt-dlp -f bestaudio -g <url>` and play it through QtMultimedia, rather than embedding a real YouTube player (Qt WebEngine/WebView — not installed on this machine, and a heavy dependency to add) or simply opening the link in the system browser (loses all in-app play/pause/stop control). This gives tight playback control tied to `PomodoroTimer` state using infrastructure (`yt-dlp`, `ffmpeg`, Qt6Multimedia) already present on the machine and already used elsewhere in the app (`SoundEffect` in `MainMenu.qml`), at the cost of depending on `yt-dlp`'s scraping of YouTube — which can break when YouTube changes its site, and which extracts audio outside YouTube's own player, outside YouTube's Terms of Service.
